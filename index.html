Common questions

Before you ask

Will testing my website break anything or take it offline?

No. Manual testing is done carefully within an agreed scope, and we avoid destructive actions. If any test could risk downtime, we'll flag it and get your approval first.

I don't know anything about cyber security — will I understand the report?

Yes. Every report is written for a business owner, not an engineer. Each issue includes what it means, how serious it is, and what to do about it — no unexplained technical terms.

How long does a scan or penetration test take?

An automated scan is typically ready within 48 hours. A manual penetration test usually takes 3–5 business days, depending on the size of your website.

Do you only work with WordPress and Shopify sites?

No — we work with any web platform, including custom-built websites, WooCommerce, Shopify, and standard hosting setups.

Why Cyffer exists

Security shouldn't be a luxury

Most cyber security firms are built for enterprises with big budgets. Cyffer was built for the small business owner who just wants their website to be safe — without the enterprise price tag.

Every day, thousands of people in India start an e-commerce business or set up a website for their shop, service, or brand. Most of them build it, launch it, and never think about security again — until something goes wrong.

By then, it's usually too late: a defaced homepage, a customer whose data leaked, a site quietly blacklisted by Google. The fix costs far more — in money and trust — than prevention ever would have.

Cyffer exists to close that gap. We bring the same testing methods used to secure large organizations, priced and explained for people running a single website — not a security team.
